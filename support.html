Installing on the Mac

You need an AI subscription: Claude with Claude Code (Pro or higher), or any ChatGPT subscription. Don’t have one? Get it first: Claude plans · ChatGPT plans.

Download Gubernos.dmg (macOS 13 or later, Apple silicon or Intel), open it and double-click Gubernos. If macOS asks whether to open an app downloaded from the Internet, choose Open. A page in your browser sets everything up, no Terminal.

Or let your agent do the setup

On your Mac, open Claude Code or Codex, whichever your plan covers (how to get one), and paste:

Install Gubernos for me: read https://raw.githubusercontent.com/old-bozman/gubernos/main/SETUP.md and do the part "For the agent".

It installs the app, creates your folder (on the Mac, or in iCloud if you’d rather), sets up the phone connection and interviews you to fill the system. It asks you for what only you can do: clicking Allow in a few dialogs, and scanning two codes with your iPhone’s camera.

Setting up the Mac by hand

The download and your agent both do this for you. By hand, follow the install guide. Your files go in ~/Gubernos unless you choose iCloud Drive or Obsidian’s folder.

Linux, or Windows

Gubernos also runs on Linux, and on Windows inside WSL (first, in PowerShell: wsl --install). In a terminal there, run:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/old-bozman/gubernos/main/install.sh | bash -s -- --wizard

It opens the same setup page as on the Mac (on WSL in the Windows browser; on a machine without a screen it prints the link). You sign in to Claude or ChatGPT on that page; with no browser on the machine, it shows the sign-in link to open on any device. It needs curl, tar and rsync. On Windows, the setup puts a small hidden starter, Gubernos.vbs, in your Startup folder so Gubernos runs after you log in; delete it to stop that. The calendar comes from Google there. More: install guide.

Connecting your iPhone

  1. Install the beta from TestFlight: how to install.
  2. On the Mac open Settings → Your Mac → iPhone and other devices → Connect your iPhone and point the iPhone camera at the code. There’s nothing to sign in to, and nothing else to install on the iPhone.
  3. Allow notifications, and Apple Health if you want habits ticked off from it.

Your Mac gets a public HTTPS address through Cloudflare, and only devices paired with its code get in. Lost a phone? On the Mac, Settings → Your Mac → iPhone and other devices → New code unpairs every device.

The traffic passes through Cloudflare under our account, and HTTPS ends there, so it could technically be seen there (what that means). To end HTTPS on your Mac, run scripts/setup.sh phone --tailscale instead, which needs a Tailscale account. To keep the Mac off the internet, run scripts/setup.sh phone --private; then the iPhone needs Tailscale too, with the same account. Both delete the Cloudflare address. To just stop it, run scripts/setup.sh stop-tunnel.

Connecting an Android phone

There’s no Android app yet, but Gubernos installs from Chrome as an app, with notifications. It needs Gubernos running on a computer: a Mac, Linux, or Windows.

  1. On that computer, in Gubernos: Settings → Your Mac → iPhone and other devices → Android or a browser. It gets an https address in about a minute.
  2. Point the phone’s camera at the code marked Android and open it in Chrome: the phone pairs and Gubernos opens. No camera handy? Copy link and send it to the phone.
  3. Chrome menu ⋮ → Install app.
  4. In Gubernos on the phone: Settings → This device → Notifications On.

Compared with the iPhone app it has no offline copy, widgets or Apple Health, and reminders come as pushes from the computer, so it needs to be on. The phone connects through the same Cloudflare address as an iPhone (what that means).

No computer?

The iPhone app also works on its own over a folder in iCloud Drive. Open Gubernos → Or use the phone on its own → Start a new system → in the file picker choose iCloud Drive → New Folder (e.g. “Gubernos”) → choose it.

That’s enough for tasks, the inbox, projects, habits, reminders, widgets and the Apple Watch. Agents, the chat and the calendar in Plan the day need Gubernos on a computer.

Connecting Google

This is optional, and only needed for agents to read Gmail, Contacts and Drive, or to use Google Calendar directly. On your Mac: Settings → Your Mac → Google → Sign in with Google. Google hasn’t verified Gubernos yet, so it warns first: Advanced → Go to Gubernos. At most 100 people can sign in until it’s verified. Rather use your own Google client? See the Google guide.

To remove access, use Settings → Your Mac → Google → Disconnect. It revokes access at Google and deletes the sign-in from your Mac. If your Mac is offline, also check myaccount.google.com/permissions. What it reads and where that goes: privacy policy.

Common questions

The app can’t read my folder / files are missing. If your folder is in iCloud, iCloud may have offloaded the files. On the Mac: Finder → the folder → right-click → Keep Downloaded. On the iPhone, open the folder once in Files.

“Can’t reach your Mac”. The Mac must be awake and online. Without the Mac, an up-to-date iPhone app still shows your files from its own copy and sends its changes when the Mac is back.

Can I bring my tasks from another app? Yes: Settings → General → Import tasks, from Todoist, Things, Apple Reminders, or any app’s export file (a TickTick CSV, for example). You see what comes in before it’s added.

Where is my data? In your folder, as plain Markdown files you can open in any editor or in Obsidian. The Mac also keeps a daily copy of them in ~/.gubernos/backups (the last 14 days and 8 weeks). See the privacy policy.
